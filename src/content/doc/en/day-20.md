With mail now mature ([day 19](/en/doc/day-19/)), the documentation had to catch up. We reordered the [tutorials](/en/tutorials/) and wrote new guides aimed at **anyone**, with no jargon.

## Organized by product

The tutorials used to be a flat list focused on Cloud. Now they are grouped by product:

- **KM0 Cloud:** how to access from the browser, Android, iOS, and macOS.
- **KM0 Mail:** the new email guides.

That way, someone looking for help finds their product at a glance, without mixing contexts.

## New KM0 Mail guides

Three short tutorials that cover the full journey of a new user:

1. **Create a KM0 Mail account:** base plan €7.99/month (mail and cloud); pick a username and password and get your `@km0digital.com` address.
2. **Sign in:** reach webmail with your address and password.
3. **Use your own domain:** end to end, add the domain, publish DNS, verify it, create addresses, and sign in with them.

Each guide gets to the point: numbered steps, one problem per block, and a common-issues section at the end.

## With screenshots where they help

Where an image saves explanation (the registration form, the sign-in screen, the domains panel), we left room for **screenshots**. They illustrate the step, not decorate the page.

## In four languages

Everything is available in Spanish, Catalan, English, and German, with the same structure, so language is not a barrier.

## Closing

The goal is for someone with no technical background to go from "I have no email" to "I write from my own domain" by following a guide. See them in [tutorials](/en/tutorials/), and if you get stuck, come to the next [meetup](/en/meeting/).

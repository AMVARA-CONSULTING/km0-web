KM0 is no longer only community and demos: **econsultants** is our first company client with full OpenCloud KM0 onboarding. It matches what we wrote on [day 22](/en/doc/day-22/): an SME with its own domain, team files, and someone who walks the handover.

## Who they are

[econsultants](https://econsultants.es/) is a renewables consultancy focused on wind and rail industry. They have spent decades connecting companies with suppliers and projects. Their day-to-day needs reliable cloud and a stable corporate site, not an opaque panel on another continent.

## What we delivered

Three pieces, one operator:

1. **OpenCloud KM0:** file cloud and collaboration on the same EU infrastructure Kilometer 0 already runs ([cloud.km0digital.com](https://cloud.km0digital.com)).
2. **Domain:** we transferred and operate **econsultants.es** on our perimeter (DNS, HTTPS, hosting).
3. **Static site:** rebuilt and published on our system. Light site, no heavy CMS: services, specialisations, and contact in ES/EN.

It was not “open an account and good luck”. It was onboarding: accounts, domain, production site, and a human counterpart when something breaks.

## Why it matters

It proves the SME path end to end: **identity and files in Cloud**, **domain under control**, **public web** in the same house. The same pattern fits other small companies that want out of “Drive + Gmail + loose hosting” without building an IT department.

Data in Germany (Hetzner), serious pricing and support: the product we already offer on [pricing](/en/pricing/), applied to a real client.

The public site had too much in the first screen and soft wording on capacity. Mid-September we shipped a clearer offer: what you get, what we mean by 150 GB, and where the tutorials stop guessing.

## What changed

- **150 GB plan.** Self-serve storage is named as 150 GB. Need more (1 TB and up)? Ask us. The Open Graph card and pricing lines match that story.
- **Leave Microsoft 365.** The hero says the job in plain words: keep mail and files, move off the locked-in suite, same EU servers and support.
- **FAQ off the home fold.** The accordion lives on `/faq/` so the home page stays product-first.
- **Sync and photo tutorials.** Desktop sync and phone photo backup now say what the public plan holds, what the apps do not do (no OneDrive-style Known Folder Move), and when to ask for more space before a big migration.

## Why it matters

SMEs and individuals both scan prices and “will my photos fit?” before they trust a contact form. Honest capacity and a clear exit from Microsoft 365 beat another privacy paragraph.

Sunday upgrades are boring on purpose. Late September we moved the Cloud stack again: OpenCloud on the rolling line into **8.0.1**, Collabora CODE on the 26.04 series, plus small fixes so office files and sync keep behaving.

## What we did

- Bumped OpenCloud through **7.5** into **8.0.1** on the rolling images we already run.
- Updated Collabora Online (CODE) so document editing stays on a current 26.04 build.
- Kept the habit: snapshot before recreate, volumes untouched, cutover when traffic is low.

## Why it is on the blog

Readers use Cloud for real folders, not for our changelog. A calm Sunday bump is part of the product: EU hosting only works if the stack stays current without drama.

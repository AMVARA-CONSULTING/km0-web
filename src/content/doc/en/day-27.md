Stock OpenCloud looks like stock OpenCloud. On 1 October we mounted a host theme so [cloud.km0digital.com](https://cloud.km0digital.com/) shows the **KM0** mark and teal lockup in the header.

## What you see

- Company logo in the top-left of the web UI (dark theme included).
- Theme colours aligned with the rest of Kilometer 0 Digital.
- No change to your folders, shares, or sync data. Branding only.

## Why

When someone opens Cloud after pricing or a meetup, the product should feel like the same house as the marketing site, not a generic demo.

Leaving Google Drive does not have to be a blind jump. A short inventory, verified copies, and a period where both places coexist lowers the risk of losing something.

## 1. List what you actually use

Open Drive and note active folders: work, photos, legal documents, shared with others. Ignore old clutter for now; you can move it later.

## 2. Download or copy in blocks

Do not try “everything at once” on a Sunday night. Copy folder by folder (or use Google Takeout if it fits). Mark each block “copied” when it finishes.

## 3. Upload to the new home and check

In the new service, recreate a simple structure (for example Documents, Photos, Shared). Open several random files. If something is missing, redo only that block.

## 4. Update links and sharing

Tell people who still have old links. Create new permissions at the destination. Meanwhile you can leave Drive read-only for a few days.

## 5. Close Drive when you are sure

After you have worked only in the new place for a while, empty or archive Drive. Keep a final export on a disk if you want a cold copy.

If any step feels overwhelming, ask a human for help: migration rewards patience, not haste.

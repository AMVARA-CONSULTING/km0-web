<section class="doc-lead-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Introduction</p>
  <p class="doc-lead">This guide gets you into KM0 Cloud from a browser. Cloud web, the desktop client, and the mobile apps share one KM0 login. You need your usual browser and your KM0 credentials.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 1</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Open the KM0 Cloud URL</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>Open your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, or another).</li>
    <li>Go to <a href="https://cloud.km0digital.com/">https://cloud.km0digital.com/</a>.</li>
    <li>If you are not signed in yet, the site sends you to the shared KM0 sign-in screen.</li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section class="doc-block doc-block-alt">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 2</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Sign in with your KM0 account</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>Enter your KM0 email or username and password.</li>
    <li>If you enabled two-factor authentication, enter the code from your authenticator app.</li>
    <li>Continue until you return to your files.</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">Detailed steps: <a href="/en/tutorials/keycloak-sign-in-cloud/">Sign in to KM0 Cloud with the shared login</a>. For 2FA setup: <a href="/en/tutorials/keycloak-2fa/">Enable 2FA on your KM0 account</a>.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 3</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">New account</h2>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">If you do not have an account yet, use the registration option on the sign-in flow, then return here after you confirm the account.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block doc-block-alt">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 4</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">After sign-in</h2>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">Once signed in, you land in your cloud space. From there you can upload files, share them, and work with your team. The same login applies when desktop or mobile asks you to authenticate.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Common problems</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Troubleshooting (web)</h2>
  <ul class="doc-list">
    <li><strong>I forgot my password:</strong> use the recovery option on the sign-in screen.</li>
    <li><strong>The page loops:</strong> clear cookies for Cloud, or try a private window, then sign in once.</li>
    <li><strong>The page does not load:</strong> check your internet connection and that the URL is exactly <code>https://cloud.km0digital.com/</code>.</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">Desktop after the login change: <a href="/en/tutorials/opencloud-desktop-relogin/">Re-login on OpenCloud Desktop</a>. Mobile: <a href="/en/tutorials/getting-started-android/">Android</a> or <a href="/en/tutorials/getting-started-ios/">iOS</a>.</p>
</section>

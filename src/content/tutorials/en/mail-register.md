<section class="doc-lead-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Introduction</p>
  <p class="doc-lead">KM0 Mail gives you your own mailbox at <strong>@km0digital.com</strong>. No technical knowledge required: just a username and a password.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 1</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Open the registration page</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>Open your usual browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, or another).</li>
    <li>Go to <a href="https://mail.km0digital.com/register">https://mail.km0digital.com/register</a>.</li>
  </ol>
  <figure class="doc-figure">
    <img src="/tutorials/mail/register-form.png" alt="KM0 Mail registration form with username, password, and confirmation fields" />
    <figcaption>Registration page at mail.km0digital.com/register.</figcaption>
  </figure>
</section>

<section class="doc-block doc-block-alt">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 2</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Choose your address</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>Type the username you want.</li>
    <li>Your address will be <code>username@km0digital.com</code>.</li>
    <li>Use only letters, numbers, and the characters <code>. _ -</code>.</li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 3</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Set a password</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>Type a password of <strong>8 characters or more</strong> with at least one symbol.</li>
    <li>Repeat it in the confirmation field.</li>
    <li>Click <strong>Create account</strong>.</li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section class="doc-block doc-block-alt">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 4</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Verify your account</h2>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">After you create the account, we take you to the sign-in page. Sign in and open the verification email in your inbox.</p>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>Sign in with your new address and password.</li>
    <li>Open the verification message and click the link.</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">You can receive and read mail right away. To <strong>send</strong> mail, you must verify the account first.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Common problems</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Troubleshooting</h2>
  <ul class="doc-list">
    <li><strong>Username already taken:</strong> choose a different username.</li>
    <li><strong>Weak password:</strong> use 8 characters or more and include a symbol.</li>
    <li><strong>No verification email:</strong> check the spam folder and wait a minute.</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">Already have an account? Learn how to sign in with the <a href="/en/tutorials/mail-sign-in/">sign-in tutorial</a>.</p>
</section>

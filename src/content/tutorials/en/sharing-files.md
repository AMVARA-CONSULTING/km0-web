<section class="doc-lead-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Introduction</p>
  <p class="doc-lead">You can share a file or folder with another KM0 Cloud user or with someone outside your organization using a public link. This guide covers both cases from the web browser.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Before you start</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Requirements</h2>
  <ul class="doc-list">
    <li>Signed in to <a href="https://cloud.km0digital.com/">KM0 Cloud</a>.</li>
    <li>Permission to share the file or folder (usually you own it or have edit access).</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">If you have not signed in yet, see the <a href="/en/tutorials/getting-started-web/">getting started in your browser</a> tutorial.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block doc-block-alt">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 1</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Open the share panel</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>Find the file or folder in your personal space or in a Space.</li>
    <li>Click the three-dot menu next to its name.</li>
    <li>Select <strong>Share</strong>.</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">A sidebar opens on the right side of the screen.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 2</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Share with someone in your organization</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>In the panel, open the <strong>Internal sharing</strong> section.</li>
    <li>Type at least three characters in the search field to find users or groups.</li>
    <li>Select the person or group from the suggestions.</li>
    <li>Choose the access level:
      <ul class="doc-list">
        <li><strong>Can view:</strong> read and download only.</li>
        <li><strong>Can upload:</strong> read and upload to shared folders.</li>
        <li><strong>Can edit:</strong> view, upload, create, change, and delete.</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>Click <strong>Share</strong>.</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">The person appears under <strong>Shared with</strong> in the same panel.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block doc-block-alt">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 3</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Share outside your organization (public link)</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>In the share panel, scroll to <strong>Public links</strong>.</li>
    <li>Click <strong>Add link</strong>.</li>
    <li>Choose the access type:
      <ul class="doc-list">
        <li><strong>Can view:</strong> view and download, no editing.</li>
        <li><strong>Can edit:</strong> view, download, edit, delete, and upload files.</li>
        <li><strong>File Drop (secret):</strong> upload only, without seeing existing content.</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>Optional: enter a password in the <strong>Password</strong> field. If the server requires one, you must set it before saving.</li>
    <li>Optional: set an <strong>Expiry date</strong> if you want the link to stop working later.</li>
    <li>Click <strong>Copy link</strong> and send the URL (and the password, if you set one) through your preferred channel.</li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 4</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Remove or change access</h2>
  <ul class="doc-list">
    <li><strong>Internal share:</strong> open <strong>Share</strong> again, find the person under <strong>Shared with</strong>, open their three-dot menu, and choose <strong>Remove share</strong>.</li>
    <li><strong>Public link:</strong> under <strong>Public links</strong>, open the link menu to change permissions, password, or expiry, or to delete it.</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">When access is removed, the person can no longer see the content immediately.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block doc-block-alt">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Common issues</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Troubleshooting</h2>
  <ul class="doc-list">
    <li><strong>User not found:</strong> make sure you typed at least three characters and that the person has a KM0 Cloud account.</li>
    <li><strong>The link asks for a password I do not remember:</strong> open the share panel, edit the link, and set a new password.</li>
    <li><strong>No Share option:</strong> you may not have permission on that file; ask the owner or Space manager for access.</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">To grant access to an entire Space (not just one file), see the <a href="/en/tutorials/space-permissions/">Space permissions tutorial</a>.</p>
</section>

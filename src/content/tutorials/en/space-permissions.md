<section class="doc-lead-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Introduction</p>
  <p class="doc-lead">A Space is your team's shared area in KM0 Cloud. If you can manage a Space, you can invite users who already exist in the system and define what they can do inside it.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Before you start</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Requirements</h2>
  <ul class="doc-list">
    <li>Signed in to <a href="https://cloud.km0digital.com/">KM0 Cloud</a>.</li>
    <li><strong>Can manage</strong> role on the Space (or you created it with that role).</li>
    <li>The person you invite must already have a KM0 Cloud account.</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">If you have not signed in yet, see the <a href="/en/tutorials/getting-started-web/">getting started in your browser</a> tutorial.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block doc-block-alt">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Available roles</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">What each member can do</h2>
  <ul class="doc-list">
    <li><strong>Can view:</strong> view and download files. Cannot upload or change anything.</li>
    <li><strong>Can edit:</strong> view, download, upload, create, change, and delete files and folders.</li>
    <li><strong>Can manage:</strong> everything above, plus invite or remove members, change Space settings, and manage quota.</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">Grant <strong>Can manage</strong> only to people you trust: they can disable or delete the Space.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 1</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Open the members menu</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>Go to the Space where you want to add people.</li>
    <li>Open the <strong>Members</strong> menu (icon or entry in the Space bar).</li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section class="doc-block doc-block-alt">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 2</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Add a member</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>In the members search field, type the user's name or email.</li>
    <li>Select them from the suggestions.</li>
    <li>Choose a role: <strong>Can view</strong>, <strong>Can edit</strong>, or <strong>Can manage</strong>.</li>
    <li>Click <strong>Add</strong>.</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">Access is immediate: the person will see the Space in their list after refreshing or signing in again.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 3</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Change a member's permissions</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>Open the <strong>Members</strong> menu again.</li>
    <li>Click the user whose role you want to change.</li>
    <li>Select the new role (<strong>Can view</strong>, <strong>Can edit</strong>, or <strong>Can manage</strong>).</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">The change applies immediately.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block doc-block-alt">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Step 4</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Remove a member</h2>
  <ol class="doc-list">
    <li>Open the <strong>Members</strong> menu.</li>
    <li>Click the three-dot menu next to the member's name.</li>
    <li>Select <strong>Remove member</strong>.</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="doc-block-intro">The person loses access to the Space immediately. Their personal files are not affected.</p>
</section>

<section class="doc-block">
  <p class="doc-block-title">Common issues</p>
  <h2 class="doc-block-heading">Troubleshooting</h2>
  <ul class="doc-list">
    <li><strong>No Members menu:</strong> your role in that Space does not include management. Ask someone with <strong>Can manage</strong> to raise your role or add the person for you.</li>
    <li><strong>User not in search:</strong> confirm they already have a KM0 Cloud account. New users must register before they can be added.</li>
    <li><strong>I only want to share one file, not the whole Space:</strong> use the <a href="/en/tutorials/sharing-files/">share files and folders tutorial</a>.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
